## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them, from the site's brand
kit:

1. **No carbon claim for the coffee.** Say "shade-grown in agroforestry, near the Mindoro Arawatan project
   landscape (VCS 6023, listed on Verra's pipeline)". Never "grown in a verified carbon project", never
   "offsets your carbon". No credits have been issued.
2. **Not "the only origin".** Say "Four species from one island, processed at origin in Roxas." Never
   "the only origin on earth producing all four species".
3. **Specific over superlative.** Give the place, altitude, month or lot code, as the site does. Honest
   about scale: small and early.
4. **The carabao means "talk to us".** Use the mascot only next to a way to talk (QR, call button,
   phone), cream on dark or espresso on cream, never smoothed or recoloured.

The call to action, as the site has it: **"Ready to source Mindoro coffee? Talk to us."** at
mindorocoffee.com.
