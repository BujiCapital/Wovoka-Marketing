# How a Mindoro Coffee video looks and sounds

Everything here follows the site's own brand kit (https://www.mindorocoffee.com/brand/) and the colour
and type tokens in https://www.mindorocoffee.com/assets/mindoro.css, both taken from the business card
printed in October 2026. If they change, they win.

## Colour

Dark and warm, like a roasted bean, with four bright cherries as the only saturated colour. Roughly 70%
espresso and roast, 25% cream, 5% cherry.

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Default background | espresso `#2A1F18` |
| Raised panels, alternate sections | roast `#33261D` |
| Text on cream (never a background) | ink `#1C1410` |
| Wordmark, text on dark, light sections | cream `#F5F0E6` |
| Secondary text and labels on dark | sand `#D8CBB6` |
| Dashed frame and hairlines only | rule `#7B736A` |
| Robusta cherry, graphic only | `#7D1C17` |
| Arabica cherry; links and accents on cream | `#A8241C` |
| Excelsa cherry; accent text on espresso | `#B9832F` |
| Liberica cherry; large accents on dark only | `#C45A24` |
| Cherry leaf and "yes" marks only | leaf `#7FA36B` |

Each species keeps its cherry colour everywhere. No gradients, gold foil effects or bright greens. No
body text in a cherry colour.

## Type

- Wordmark and headlines: **DM Serif Display**, regular weight (never bold), title case. Tight leading.
- Labels, data, buttons: **JetBrains Mono**, uppercase, tracked 0.1 to 0.3em.
- Paragraphs only: **DM Sans**.

All three are free on Google Fonts. No fourth typeface.

## On-screen data

- Facts go in JetBrains Mono: numbers, coordinates, altitudes, lot codes, grades.
- Spec-sheet style from the card back: label left, value right, uppercase, solid rule lines between
  groups. For example `SPECIES  EXCELSA`, `ORIGIN  ROXAS, OR. MINDORO`.
- The dashed origin tag (2 px dashed rule colour, 6 px corners) frames one thing per view.
- Photo captions in mono: subject, place and altitude, month and year, as in
  `Cherries under canopy · Bongabong, 671 ft · June 2026`.
- Photos: canopy, cherries on the branch, drying beds, green beans, the hulling line, the road to the
  farm. Hands at work with no face visible. No identifiable people, never Indigenous community members.
  No stock coffee from other countries, no latte art.

## Voice

Like someone who has been to the farm this week: plain, specific, a little proud, never salesy. The
card says it in eight words: "Four species. One island." Field, not boardroom. Short sentences. A number
we can show beats an adjective we can't.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: the primary lockup on espresso (cherry row, "Mindoro Coffee", coordinate line), then
`mindorocoffee.com` in JetBrains Mono and one line: "Four species. One island. Talk to us." Hold for two
seconds.
