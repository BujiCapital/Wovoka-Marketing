## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **No faces in public.** No photos of people at all, not only community members. Landscapes, cloud,
   satellite views, maps and phone screens only.
2. **State the limits the site states.** Ghost Dance does not issue, rate or certify carbon credits. It is
   not third-party verification. It does not replace a community's own consent process. Never say or
   imply otherwise.
3. **No pay or reward talk in marketing.** No amounts, no "earn", no referral pitch. Say what the record
   is, not what anyone gets paid.
4. **No project, place or partner names** unless they are in `messages.md` with a public source.

The call to action, as the site has it: **Partner with Wovoka** (the main button), with "Open the app"
as the second.
