# How a Ghost Dance video looks and sounds

The site has two looks, and a video can use both: **instrument** (near-black, sans and mono readouts,
thin hairlines, small corner ticks) and **ground** (cream paper, serif headlines).

## Colour

Taken from the stylesheet the site serves.

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Dark ground ("orbit") | near-black `#08100B` |
| Panels on dark | `#0F1F16` |
| The icon tile, green buttons | forest green `#1A3C2A` |
| The one highlight: the key line, the key number | gold `#E8B04B` |
| Text on dark; paper ground | cream `#F4EBDD` |
| Text on paper | ink `#16211B` |
| Secondary text on paper | `#5E6B62` |
| A tick or "checked" mark only | soft green `#8FD6A8`, small, never as decoration |

One gold thing per frame. Everything else is cream on near-black, or ink on cream.

## Type

- Headlines and the wordmark: a serif, weight 600. The site uses the reader's system serif (Iowan Old
  Style, Palatino, Georgia). For video and graphics use **Noto Serif** 600, the Google Font in the site's
  own list. (starter: confirm)
- Body text: the site uses the system sans. For video use **Noto Sans**, also in the site's own list.
  (starter: confirm)
- Readouts, labels, dates and source lines: **IBM Plex Mono**, uppercase with wide letter spacing. This is
  the one web font the site loads.

All three are free from Google Fonts.

## On-screen text and data

- Labels and readouts in IBM Plex Mono capitals, small, with a thin hairline, like the site's
  "CLOUD MASKED · MEDIAN PER PIXEL".
- Corner ticks (small L-shapes) may frame a map, a phone or a record card.
- Every satellite image carries its credit line in mono, bottom left: "Contains modified Copernicus
  Sentinel data", with the years.
- Every number carries a source stamp with its date, and comes from `messages.md` (still to write).
- Good b-roll: satellite passes clearing, the offline map, phone screens, empty landscapes. No people.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (type, maps and satellite views under
  a voice-over in Lee's voice).
- Lower third on first appearance: "Lee Pearson, founder, Wovoka". (starter: confirm)
- A small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, map or satellite view on the right. Plain near-black background.

## Voice

Taken from the site's own copy. Plain and exact. Says what can and cannot be seen. Short sentences, often
in pairs: "Satellites see the land. The people on it know it." States its own limits openly. Names the
data and the date. No hype, no exclamation marks, no promises.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: icon and wordmark on near-black (`logo-on-dark.png`), then the site's line in serif, with the
second sentence in gold: "Satellites see the land. **The people on it know it.**" Under it `ghost.dance`
in IBM Plex Mono. Hold for two seconds. For partners, the action is "Partner with Wovoka".
