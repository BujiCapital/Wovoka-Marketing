# How a Wovoka Advisory video looks and sounds

Taken from the new build of www.wovokagroup.com and its stylesheet, 19 Oct 2026. The served URLs below
work after the domain cutover; until then the old site may answer at www.wovokagroup.com.

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Dark sections, footer, buttons, end cards | forest `#14402c` |
| Links and rule lines on paper; button hover | forest mid `#1d5c3e` |
| Pale panels (quotes, notes) | forest low `#eaf1ec` |
| Page ground | paper `#fbfaf7` (warm bands `#f4f1ea`) |
| Text on paper | ink `#12211a` (secondary `#3d5147`, muted `#6b7f74`) |
| Small labels on dark | sage `#8fb8a1` (text on dark `#cfe0d5`) |
| The one warm accent: small case-study labels | clay `#b4552d` |

The site's own note: three greens and one warm accent. Keep clay small.

## Type

- Headlines: **Spectral**, weight 500 (serif).
- Text: **Inter** (400, 500, 600).
- Small uppercase labels, figures and dates: **IBM Plex Mono** 500, wide letter spacing.
- All three free from Google Fonts. The logo's own lettering is not Spectral; never retype the logo.

## On-screen text and data

- A small uppercase label in IBM Plex Mono above the headline, as the site does:
  "NATURAL CAPITAL · PHILIPPINES · UGANDA · EMERGING MARKETS".
- Headline in Spectral, ink on paper or white on forest.
- Figures and dates in IBM Plex Mono. Use only figures the site states, in its words.
- Drone and landscape footage of degraded and restored land is the house look (the hero is an aerial of
  mangrove regrowth on tidal flats). No people from project sites, no client logos.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (type, aerial footage and maps under
  a voice-over in Lee's voice).
- Lower third on first appearance: "Lee Pearson, founder".
- A small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, map or aerial on the right. Plain forest green or paper
  background.

## Voice

The voice of someone who has done the fieldwork and will tell you the inconvenient answer. Plain, exact,
unhurried. Lead with what was found on the ground, then what it means for the decision. The site says it
best: "Most of our findings are inconvenient." No hype, no exclamation marks, no promise that a project
will work.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: the white logo on forest `#14402c`, then `wovokagroup.com` and the line from the site's title:
"Ground truth for nature-based investment." Then "Start a conversation · hello@wovokagroup.com". Hold for
two seconds.
