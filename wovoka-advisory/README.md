## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **Sister firm, not subsidiary.** Say "Wovoka Advisory". When Wovoka comes up, it is "our sister firm".
   Never "part of Wovoka" or "Wovoka's advisory arm".
2. **Never name a client**, in words, logos or captions. The site's case studies are anonymous; keep
   them that way.
3. **Keep the site's claims as they are.** Carbon and land figures are audited by the people who hire us.
   Use only what the site says, in its words.
4. **Say what does not work.** The site's position is that the useful answer is often "not here" or "not
   yet". Never promise that a project will be financeable.
