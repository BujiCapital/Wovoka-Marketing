# Carbon.Supply video scripts for HeyGen

First drafts, 13 Oct 2026. Every script is a draft until it is approved on the content desk. Numbers carry
their fact ID from [`../messages.md`](../messages.md): re-check each one on the day of the render and update
the date in the source stamp.

**For every script:** presenter is Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice (see
[`../brand.md`](../brand.md)), head and shoulders on the left third, near-black background, data on the
right. Burned-in captions in IBM Plex Sans. Source stamp in IBM Plex Mono, bottom left, whenever a number is
on screen. End card as in `brand.md`. Spoken lines run about 2.5 words a second.

B-roll clips named below are in [`../b-roll/`](../b-roll/) (`landscape/` and `portrait/`).

Render each in 1:1 first (LinkedIn and Facebook feed), then 9:16 from the same scenes.

|---|---|---|

| 15–20 s | Why it matters to the reader of that edition, in one sentence, with no rating or recommendation | |
| 20–25 s | "The full story is free at carbon dot supply." | End card |

Take facts only from the published story page and list each one, with its link, alongside the draft.
