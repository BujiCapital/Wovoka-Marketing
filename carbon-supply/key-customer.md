# The key customer

One person to picture when writing a script. A composite, not a real customer.

## Who they are

**A deal or origination lead focused on one region**, at a project developer, a regional fund or an advisory
firm. They cover, say, Southeast Asia nature-based projects, or cookstoves in East Africa. Small team, no
data department. They read registries themselves today: Verra's search, Gold Standard's, a spreadsheet they
update when they remember.

## What their week looks like

- Monday: someone asks "anything new in Indonesia?" They spend an hour on registry searches to answer.
- Mid-week: a call with a developer. They look up the project ID ten minutes before.
- Friday: they hear from a newsletter or LinkedIn that a project changed hands weeks ago. They missed it.

## What they want

- To hear about a listing, a status change or a delisting **in their region, the week it happens**.
- A project's full status history on one page, with a link they can paste into a memo.
- To ask a quick question ("is 5584 registered yet?") and get the answer with the source.
- To buy it themselves, on a card, without a procurement process or a sales call.

## What they don't want

- A whole-market platform priced for a large team.
- A rating or an opinion. They form their own; they need the facts fast.
- Hype. They know the market better than most people writing about it.

## What wins them

1. A video that shows a real registry move they did not know about (scripts 2 and 3).
2. Seeing their region or project type as its own edition.
3. A free weekly read that is useful from the first issue.

## What we ask them to do

Get the free weekly read at carbon.supply. Then look up one project they care about.

Other audiences (investors with a regional thesis, consultants, development-finance teams, corporate
credit buyers) are in [`audiences.md`](audiences.md).
