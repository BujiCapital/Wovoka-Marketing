## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **The call to action is the free weekly read at carbon.supply.** Paid plans are published at
   carbon.supply/pricing/ but are not open yet, so no "buy now".
2. **Report, never rate.** We say what the registries publish. No "good" or "bad" project, no "buy" or
   "avoid".
3. **Every number on screen carries a source stamp** with its date.
