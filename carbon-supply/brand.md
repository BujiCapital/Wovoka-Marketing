# How a Carbon.Supply video looks and sounds

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Background | near-black `#0b0e0c` |
| The only brand colour: logo, highlights, the key number | gold `#c4a35a` |
| Paper, light cards, captions on dark | cream `#f5f2eb` |
| Status only (for example "issued", "registered") | green, small, never as decoration |

One gold thing per frame. Everything else is cream or grey on near-black.

## Type

- Headlines and UI text: **IBM Plex Sans** (600 for the wordmark).
- Numbers, registry IDs, dates, source stamps: **IBM Plex Mono**.
- Long headlines from a story may use **Fraunces**.

## Logo

| File | Use in video |
|---|---|
| https://carbon.supply/brand/logo-on-dark.png | End card on near-black |
| https://carbon.supply/brand/logo-on-light.png | End card on cream |
| https://carbon.supply/brand/mark.svg | Small corner bug, top left |
| https://carbon.supply/brand/profile-1024.png | Channel avatars |

The mark is "the Bracket": a square gold C with a cream dot. Written **Carbon.Supply** (capital C, capital
S). Spoken "carbon dot supply".

## Charts and data on screen

- Every chart or number carries a source stamp in Plex Mono, bottom left:
  `Source: Verra registry via carbon.supply · 13 Oct 2026`.
- Show registry IDs as the registry writes them ("Verra 5584", "GS23612").
- Screen recordings of the live site (home page tape, /latest/, a project page, a data story) are the best
  b-roll: real rows, real dates. Record at 1080 wide, scroll slowly, crop to the rows that matter.
- Landscape photos already on the site are fine. No photos with identifiable people.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (type, charts and screen recordings
  under a voice-over in Lee's voice).
- Lower third on first appearance: "Lee Pearson, founder".
- A small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, data on the right. Plain near-black background.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. A registry analyst explaining what moved, not an advert. Short sentences. One idea a
scene. Lead with the fact, then what it means, then where to read more. No exclamation marks.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: logo on near-black, then `carbon.supply` in Plex Sans and one line:
"The free weekly read. What moved on the registries." Hold for two seconds.
