# What Carbon.Supply is

## One line

**Carbon.Supply reads the carbon registries every day and tells you what moved in the part of the market you
work in.**

Longer: a data desk and AI analyst for the carbon market. We read Verra, Gold Standard, Isometric and
Equitable Earth every morning, keep every day's snapshot, and send you the changes in your region or project
type, each linked to the registry record. (Wording from https://carbon.supply/why/.)

## What a reader gets

| Piece | In plain words | Where |
|---|---|---|

| Editions | The weekly read cut to one region or one project type. Eleven of them, stackable | carbon.supply/why/ |

The eleven editions. Regions: Southeast Asia, East Africa, Brazil & the Amazon, India, Rest of world.
Project types (global): Blue carbon, Rice & AWD, Forests (ARR, REDD+, IFM), Cookstoves, Super pollutants,
Durable removal (CDR).

## Why it is different

- **The change, not the table.** A registry shows today's state. We keep every day and show what moved, from
  what to what.
- **Before the press release.** Announcements come weeks after the registry moves. We watch the registry.
- **The slice you need.** One region or one project type, not the whole market.
- **A published price, no sales call.** Ours is on the page.
- **Every answer links to the record.** You can check it yourself.

## What it is not

- **Not a rating.** We report what registries publish. Where a classification is ours (project type, status
  grouping) it is labelled as ours. We do not score projects or tell anyone what to buy.
- Not investment advice.
- No satellite analysis and no proprietary ratings. Say so plainly.
