# Guardrails: check every script against this list

A script that breaks one of these is not approved.

## Never say or show

- **A partnership or endorsement** from any institution, registry, standard body or company that is not
  public and confirmed. Do not name readers, clients or institutions who use Carbon.Supply.
- **A number without a source** in `messages.md`, or a count that was not re-checked on the day.
- **A rating or a recommendation.** No "good project", "bad project", "safe", "buy", "avoid". A late
  verification is a registry fact, not a verdict on a developer.
- **Why a named company did something.** Say what changed on the registry (proponent, validator, estimate),
  not the reason. Do not name the companies involved in a change unless the script already does.
- **Carbon claims beyond the registry's own words.** Use the registry's status names. Estimated tonnes are
  what a project "claims", never what it "delivers" or "removes".
- **People.** No photos, footage or generated images of community members or any identifiable person,
  except Lee's own avatar.
- **"Independent"** or "we don't sell credits".
- **A sales push.** The call to action is the free weekly read. A price may be quoted from /pricing/ as a
  fact, never as "buy now" or a deadline.
- **Other businesses.** Carbon.Supply videos are about Carbon.Supply only.
- **Investment talk**: returns, yields, projections, offers.
- **Competitors run down.** Another company may be named only in a neutral comparison whose every claim is
  checkable on its own website.

## Always

- The source stamp on every chart or number on screen.
- The date a count was true.
- "AI analyst" when the WhatsApp analyst is mentioned.
- Music licensed for commercial use.
- An approval on the content desk before anything is published.
