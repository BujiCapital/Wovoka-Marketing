# Carbon.Supply b-roll and product demos

Screen recordings of the live site, made 13 Oct 2026 with a scripted browser, so the rows, dates and counts
are real. No people appear in any clip. Use them behind Lee's avatar or as full-screen cutaways.

- `landscape/`: 1920×1080, for 16:9, and for 1:1 crops (crop the centre).
- `portrait/`: 1080×1920, the phone layout of the site, for Reels, Shorts and Stories.
- MP4 (H.264), 30 fps, no sound, 11–23 seconds each.

## How to use b-roll in HeyGen

1. Download the clips and drag them into HeyGen (its chat or media library), or upload them through the HeyGen
   API. HeyGen cannot import MP4 or PNG files from GitHub links; it reads the Markdown files from raw links
   only.
2. Layout: Lee's avatar on the left third, the clip on the right two-thirds; or the clip full screen with
   Lee's voice over it.
3. Put the on-screen text from the script over the clip, and the source stamp bottom left.
4. Trim to the moment that matters: two to four seconds a cut is plenty.
5. For a phone-first video, use `portrait/` full screen with Lee in a circle bottom-right.
