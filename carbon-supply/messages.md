# Key messages and the facts behind them

Every number in a video, caption or post must be in the facts table below, with its public source. Counts
change daily: **re-check a count on the day the video is made** and say the date ("as of 13 October").
When in doubt, round down and say "more than".

## Five messages

1. **We read the registries every day, so you know before the market does.**
   Announcements come weeks after the registry moves. We watch the registry itself.
2. **The change, not the table.** Registries show today. We keep every day and show what moved, from what to
   what, with a link to the record.
3. **Pay for the slice you need.** Eleven editions, one region or one project type each. Start free.
4. **Your carbon analyst on WhatsApp.** Ask for this week's market summary for your region, or the status of
   every project on your deal list, and get the answer with the registry link.
5. **The quiet rows are the interesting ones.** Delistings, relistings and late verifications are never
   announced. They are where the stories are.

## Words

Say: registry, listing, status, pipeline, late to verify, edition, the weekly read, the record.
Avoid: "trust", "operating system", "AI-native", "independent", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock".
