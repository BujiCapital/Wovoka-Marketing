# Who we are talking to

People deploying into **one region or one project type** who need registry facts every week, without paying
for a whole-market platform.

| Audience | What they ask | What makes them act | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project developers | Who else is listing in my region? What did that methodology change do? | Seeing a move before it is announced | "A developer listed a third project in Kalimantan. No press release." |
| Investors with a regional thesis | What is in the pipeline in my region? | A project they did not know about | "Nobody announces a missed verification." |
| Consultants and advisers | What is the status of project X today, with a link I can cite? | A free project page that answers in one screen | "Look up any project, free." |
| Development-finance and NGO teams | What's on the registry, who's done what, is anything new? | The weekly read in their inbox | "What moved on the registries this week." |
| Corporate credit buyers | Which projects carry CORSIA, Article 6 or CCP labels? | Labels shown exactly as the registry publishes them | "The market no longer runs on one label." |

The question every audience shares: **what's on the registry, who's done what, is there anything new?**

## Tone

They read registries for a living or work next to people who do. Talk to them as peers: specific, short, no
hype. A project ID and a date persuade more than an adjective.

## What we want them to do

1. **Get the free weekly read** at carbon.supply. The call to action in every video.
2. Look up a project they care about.
3. Turn on WhatsApp answers at carbon.supply/whatsapp/.
