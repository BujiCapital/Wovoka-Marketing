# Who wovoka.org is for

| Audience | What they ask | What makes them act | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| General readers of history | What really happened in the West? | One striking object with a plain caption | A map or a page of a report they have never seen |
| Teachers and students | Where is the primary source? | The source named, with a link to read it | "Every source is listed, most of them free to read online" |
| People who know Wounded Knee but not Wovoka | Who was he? | His message in his own words | "Do not fight, love one another, do right" |
| Readers interested in land, water and food | How was the land taken? | Concrete numbers from the record | 85 buffalo left; two million cords of wood burned |
| Lee's LinkedIn network | Why does your company carry this name? | Lee, first person, plainly | Why a company carries his name |

## Tone

Plain and careful. Say what the sources say and no more. No adjectives doing the work the facts should do.
No outrage bait, no "you won't believe". Hard testimony is quoted in full, in text, never as a pull quote
over a picture.

## The action we want

Read the page. Then read the source.
