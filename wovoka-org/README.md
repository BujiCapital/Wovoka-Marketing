# wovoka.org marketing

Start here if you are making a post or a video for wovoka.org (https://www.wovoka.org). First version
19 Oct 2026. Everything here is a draft and changes by pull request.

wovoka.org is a history site, not a product. It tells the story of Wovoka (Jack Wilson, c. 1856 to 1932),
the Northern Paiute man of Mason Valley, Nevada, whose 1889 vision of peace crossed the American West,
and of what was done to the land, water and peoples of the West in his lifetime. It is written for the
general reader and built from the published record, with every source listed.

## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **One sourced object a day.** Each post is one thing from the site (a map, a page of a report, a
   quote, a song as published) with the site's own caption and credit, and a link to the page it is on.
2. **The site's words, not ours.** Every fact, date, number, name and quote comes from a live page and
   matches it word for word inside quotes. If it is not on the site, it is not in a post.
3. **No presenter.** No avatar, no AI voice, no generated images. This rule overrides the portfolio rule
   that allows Lee's avatar: this brand uses none. A human narrator may be added later; ask Lee.
4. **No people in pictures.** No photographs of Native people, historic ones included. Landscapes, maps,
   documents, drawings.
5. **No company.** No money, carbon, fundraising, investor or product language, and no other Wovoka brand.
6. **The call to action is reading the page.** No sign-up, no donation, no "follow us".

| Threads (northwest_lee) | Every day, 14:00 UTC: a short version, under 500 characters |
| Lee Pearson's LinkedIn | About twice a week, 15:30 UTC: first person, the strongest pieces only. Lee may say his company carries the name; nothing more about the company |

Never on Carbon.Kids, Carbon Supply or Buji Vanilla channels: they have other audiences.

Links carry `?utm_source=linkedin|threads|linkedin-lee&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=museum-launch`.

Sizes: 4:5 (1080×1350) for films on LinkedIn and Threads; pictures as the site shows them; 1200×630
share cards for link previews.
