# The one reader to picture

**Who:** a retired schoolteacher in her sixties, in Ohio or Oregon, who reads history on her phone in the
evening. She knows Wounded Knee from a book she read years ago. She has never heard of Wovoka.

**Her week:** LinkedIn and Threads in short bursts. She stops for a picture she has not seen before and
reads a caption if it is plain and short enough.

**What she wants:** to understand what actually happened, from someone who shows their sources.

**What she does not want:** a lecture, a campaign, a sales pitch, or anyone telling her how to feel.

**What wins her:** one real object (a map, a report page, a song as it was written down), a plain caption,
and a link that leads to more of the same. She shares it with a grandchild doing a school project.
