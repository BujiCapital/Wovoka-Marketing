# How wovoka.org looks and sounds

## Colour

From the site's stylesheet. The site is quiet: paper, ink, one earth accent.

| Use | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Paper (background) | `#f6f2ea` | `#1b1916` |
| Second paper (cards) | `#ece6da` | `#24211d` |
| Ink (text) | `#23201c` | `#ece6da` |
| Second ink (captions) | `#5a544b` | `#b3aa9c` |
| Accent (links, the key date) | `#7a4a2a` | `#d9a27a` |
| Second accent (land, maps) | `#4f5d3f` | `#a9b893` |

## Type

- Headlines and the wordmark: **Fraunces** (400 and 600).
- Body, captions, credits: **Inter Tight** (400 to 600).
- Both from Google Fonts.

## Wordmark

"Wovoka" set in Fraunces 600. There is no logo file. Write the site as **wovoka.org** in posts.

## Pictures

- Use only pictures the site already shows, with its caption: landscapes, maps, government documents,
  drawings, astronomical photographs. The share cards in [`brand-kit/`](brand-kit/) are ready to use.
- The hero is Mooney's January 1892 photograph of Mason Valley, public domain.
- Films are recorded from the live pages (see [`video/scripts.md`](video/scripts.md)): the page's own
  maps, its own captions, no added effects.

## Captions in posts

End with "Pictured:" and the site's caption and credit, then the link. For example:
"Pictured: Mason Valley, Nevada, where he lived, photographed in January 1892. Public domain."

## Sound

Films are silent with burned-in text. No music for now. No AI voice. A human narrator may come later.
