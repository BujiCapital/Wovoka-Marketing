## Logo: the hat

Lee chose the mark on 19 Oct 2026: a drawing of Wovoka's tall, creased, wide-brimmed hat. The site shows no
photographs of him, and the hat is how people knew him: in nearly every photograph he wears a Stetson, and Howard
Rogers remembered that he wore a hat nobody else wore (as told in Michael Hittman, Wovoka and the Ghost Dance, 1990, pp. 110 to 111). When Mooney met him in 1892 the hat was "a broad white hat".

|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | The hat in ink on light grounds |
| `mark-light.svg` | The hat in paper on dark grounds |
| `mark-square.svg`, `favicon.svg` | Square tile and favicon |
| `profile-1024.png`, `profile-dark-1024.png` | Channel profile pictures (circle-crop safe) |
| `lockup.png`, `lockup-dark.png` | The hat with the wordmark "Wovoka" in Fraunces 600 |
| `eclipse.svg`, `eclipse-dark.svg`, `eclipse-1024.png`, `eclipse-dark-1024.png` | The hat before the ring of the 1 January 1889 eclipse, the day of his vision. For share cards, title cards and the last frame of films, not as the everyday logo |

One colour only: ink or paper. The band is a gap, not a second colour. The eclipse ring is the only place the
accent appears. Do not add a feather, beadwork or anything else to the hat, and never put a face under it.

### The hat film (`brand-kit/motion/`)

A 10-second animation of the mark, silent. The black hat tumbles and stars fall out of it, a comet crosses,
the galaxy turns, then the hat turns face-on in front of the sun and becomes the 1889 eclipse, and spins down
to land in front of the ring with the wordmark. It is a coded 3D drawing, not AI-generated.

| File | Use |

| `hat-film-1x1.mp4` | 1080×1080, square posts and channel intros |

**Held: do not post it yet.** Lee is saving it for a launch (20 Oct 2026); it goes out first with that launch,
and this line will say when it is free to use. After that it can open or close the channel's own films. It is a
logo, not history: never caption it as a picture of his real hat or of the eclipse.

Write the site as **wovoka.org** in posts.

## Pictures

- Use only pictures the site already shows, with its caption: landscapes, maps, government documents,
  drawings, astronomical photographs. The share cards in [`brand-kit/`](brand-kit/) are ready to use.
- The hero is Mooney's January 1892 photograph of Mason Valley, public domain.
- Films are recorded from the live pages (see [`video/scripts.md`](video/scripts.md)): the page's own
  maps, its own captions, no added effects. The hat film is the one exception, because it is the logo.

## Captions in posts

End with "Pictured:" and the site's caption and credit, then the link. For example:
"Pictured: Mason Valley, Nevada, where he lived, photographed in January 1892. Public domain."

## Sound

Films are silent with burned-in text. No music for now. No AI voice. A human narrator may come later.
