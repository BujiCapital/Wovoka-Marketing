# Key messages and facts

## Messages

1. **A vision of peace.** Wovoka told everyone who came to him the same thing: do not fight, love one
   another, do right.
2. **What happened to their world.** In his lifetime the land, the water, the buffalo, the pine nuts and
   the fish were taken. That is why so many people staked everything on his words.
3. **The record shows it.** Maps, reports and first-hand accounts, every one listed and most free to read.
4. **Not the end of the story.** He lived another forty-two years after Wounded Knee, and his people did
   not let him be forgotten.
5. **"There are no photographs of Wovoka or of any Native person here, by choice."** (the site, /)
