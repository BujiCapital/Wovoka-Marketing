# Guardrails: check every post against this list

A post that breaks one of these is not approved. This brand is stricter than the others because it is about
real peoples, their dead and their sacred practice.

## Never say or show

- **Photographs of Native people**, historic ones included. No portraits of Wovoka.
- **AI**: no generated images, voices or avatars, and no Lee avatar.
- **Anything not on the site.** No fact, number, date, name or quote that is not on a live page.
- **An endorsement.** Never say or imply that any tribe endorsed, reviewed or approved the site.
- **Graves and burial places.** No post about his grave or the cemetery, no pictures of it, no directions.
- **Ghost Dance song recordings.** Song texts only, as James Mooney published them in 1896, with the source.
- **Sacred material beyond what is published.** Nothing told privately or unpublished.
- **Hard testimony as a picture.** Accounts of killing (Wounded Knee, the California killings) are never a
  pull quote over an image. On the Wounded Knee anniversary, 29 December, one plain text post or none, as
  Lee decides.
- **The company.** No money, carbon, fundraising, investor or product language; no other Wovoka brand or
  website. Lee's own posts may say his company carries the name.
- **Outrage bait.** No "shocking", "you won't believe", no emojis on hard subjects.

## Always

- The site's caption and credit on every picture ("Public domain", "No known restrictions" as the site has it).
- Quotes word for word, with any cut marked.
- Where sources disagree (his birth year, the date of his death), say so.
- A link to the page the post comes from.
- A check of every fact against the page on the day.
