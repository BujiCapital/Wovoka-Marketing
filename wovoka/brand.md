# How a Wovoka video looks and sounds

Taken from the rebuilt pages of www.wovoka.io (home, /our-model, /carbon-market) and their stylesheet,
19 Oct 2026. Older pages on the site still carry the previous design; do not copy from them.

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Darkest ground: hero, footer, end cards | green night `#141c10` |
| Dark sections and cards | deep green `#22301b` |
| Page ground, light cards | paper `#f6f5ef` (sand `#ebe6d8` for alternate bands) |
| Text on paper | ink `#17200f` (secondary text muted `#5b6455`) |
| Small labels on paper | green `#4c653d` |
| The accent on dark grounds: a key number, a rule line | gold `#d8b46a` |
| The accent on paper | gold `#b48a3c` |

Mostly green and paper. Gold is used sparingly, for one thing a frame. (starter: confirm)

## Type

- Headlines: **Barlow**, weight 500, tight tracking.
- Text, labels and buttons: **Inter Tight** (400 for text, 600 for small uppercase labels).
- Both free from Google Fonts. The site has no monospace face; use Inter Tight with tabular figures for
  numbers.

## On-screen text and data

- A small uppercase label above each headline, in Inter Tight 600 with wide letter spacing, as the site's
  share cards do: "NATURAL CAPITAL OPERATOR", "CARBON MARKET · SOUTHEAST ASIA".
- Headline in Barlow, white on a dark landscape photo or green night.
- `wovoka.io` small, bottom right, as on the share cards.
- Every registry fact names the registry, the ID and the date checked, as the site's "On the record"
  strip does: `Verra · VCS 6023 · checked 8 Oct 2026`. Numbers come from `messages.md` once it exists.
- Landscapes, crops and drone shots only. No people from the project sites.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (type, landscapes and screen
  recordings under a voice-over in Lee's voice).
- Lower third on first appearance: "Lee Pearson, founder".
- A small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, landscape or data on the right. Plain green night background.

## Voice

Proof before story. The site is where people check that what they heard is real, so every claim is one
they can find on wovoka.io with a name or a registry ID. Plain, calm, specific. "Carbon claims change only
when the registry does. We quote it and date the check." Short sentences, no exclamation marks, no money
language.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: the white logo on green night `#141c10`, then `wovoka.io` and the site's hero line:
"Building yield from restored tropical land." Hold for two seconds. Where the video asks for action, add
"Partner with us · wovoka.io/partner".
