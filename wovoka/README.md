## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **The site makes no offer.** "Yield" means what restored land produces, never a figure or a promise.
   No "invest", no returns, no funding targets. The site's footer line applies to every post: it is not an
   offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security.
2. **Quote the registry in its own words, with the date checked.** The Mindoro project is "listed on
   Verra's pipeline". Never say "registered", "verified" or "validated" until the registry does.
3. **Wovoka Advisory is our sister firm, a separate company.** Its feasibility and field work for
   government and multilaterals is not Wovoka's project work. Never present it as Wovoka's.
4. **Communities hold the land.** Say "built with the communities who hold the land", as the site does.
   No photos of people from the projects: landscapes, crops and data only.

## Call to action

As the site has it: **Partner with us** at wovoka.io/partner (companies, funders, carbon credit buyers,
researchers). For landholders in the Philippines: **Tell us about the land** at wovoka.io/scope-your-land.
Email: restore@wovoka.io.
