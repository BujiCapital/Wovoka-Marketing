# How an Inclusive Forests video looks and sounds

The site is light: warm cream pages, deep forest green headlines, landscape photos with white type on top,
and a deep green footer.

## Colour

Taken from the site's stylesheets (the `:root` colours on its pages).

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Headlines, footer, dark ground | forest deep `#1A3C2A` |
| Second green, gradients | forest mid `#2D6A4F` |
| Buttons and highlights | leaf green `#52B788` |
| Second accent: send buttons, outlines | sun orange `#E67E22` |
| Small accents | teal `#2A9D8F`, amber `#D4A259`, earth brown `#6B4226` |
| Page ground | cream `#FAF7F2` |
| Body text | charcoal `#2C2C2C` |

Forest deep and cream carry most frames. Use leaf green for the one thing to look at. Teal, amber and
earth brown are small accents only, never large areas.

## Type

- Headlines and the wordmark: **Playfair Display**, 700.
- Body text and labels: **DM Sans**.

Both are free from Google Fonts and are the fonts the site loads.

## On-screen text and data

- Headlines in Playfair Display, short labels and captions in DM Sans.
- White type on a landscape photo needs a dark shadow or a deep green band, as on the site's hero.
- Every number carries a source stamp with its date, and comes from `messages.md` (still to write).
- Good b-roll: forests, coasts, rivers, mangroves, wildlife and crops. No people.

## The presenter

- The site does not name a founder or any person. Default to **no presenter**: type and landscapes under
  a voice-over. (starter: confirm)
- If Lee decides to appear, it is **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, with a small caption
  "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance. The lower-third wording is for Lee to set.

## Voice

Taken from the site's own copy. Warm, direct and rights-first. Lead with the people who hold the land:
"Their Land. Their Forest. Their Future." Short headlines, plain sentences, no jargon. Speaks about
capacity and working alongside communities, never about saving them. No exclamation marks.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: mark and wordmark on forest deep (`logo-on-dark.png`), then the site's line in Playfair Display:
"Their Land. Their Forest. Their Future." Under it `inclusiveforests.org` in DM Sans. Hold for two seconds.
