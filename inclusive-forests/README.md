## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **No solicitation.** Inclusive Forests is a nonprofit. Show the work and how to get in touch; never ask
   for money in a post or video. No "donate now", no amounts, no appeals.
2. **Communities speak for themselves.** Say "with communities" and "on their own terms", as the site does.
   Never speak for a named community, and never show community members.
3. **Credit stock photos.** Some photos on the site are stock and need their credit line. Use only the files
   in `brand-kit/`, or check the credit before using any other photo from the site.
4. **Only links the site makes.** The site does not name any other company or person, so neither do we.

The call to action, as the site has it: **For Communities** and **For Donors** (the two hero buttons, each
leading to a contact form), or write to hello@inclusiveforests.org.
