# How a Buji Vanilla video looks and sounds

## Colour

From the custom properties in the site's CSS (`index.html` on bujivanilla.com).

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| The brand colour: headings, links, nav on light | green `#405E38` |
| Dark ground, footer, end cards | deep green `#23331f` (darkest: `#16210f`) |
| The "Shop on Amazon" button only | brown `#855832` |
| Page ground, logo on dark | cream `#DFDFD8` |
| Light cards, text on dark | paper `#f5f4ec` |
| Body text, logo on light | ink `#11151C` |

Mostly green and cream, like the site. Brown is kept for the buy button, so use it for the call to
action and nothing else (starter: confirm).

## Type

- Headlines: **Platypi** (600, italic for short accent lines such as "Vanilla").
- Body text and labels: **Mulish** (400 to 700; labels uppercase, widely tracked).
- The one big hero word ("REAL") on the site is **Archivo Black**, outlined. Use it for one word per
  video at most (starter: confirm).

All three are free on Google Fonts.

## Logo

| File | Use in video |
|---|---|
| https://bujivanilla.com/images/logo-cream.png | Logo on dark green or on a dark photo: end cards, corner bug |
| https://bujivanilla.com/images/logo-dark.png | Logo on cream or paper |

Copies are in [`brand-kit/logos/`](brand-kit/logos/) as `buji-logo-cream.png` and `buji-logo-dark.png`.
The files are small (247×200). Ask for a larger or vector logo before using it big (starter: confirm).

The logo is the word "Buji" in script. Written **Buji Vanilla** in text (capital B, capital V); the site
and share cards also use plain **Buji**. Spoken "BOO-jee" (starter: confirm).

## On-screen text

- Short lines in Platypi over product or landscape shots, as on the share card: "REAL UGANDAN VANILLA",
  then "Single-origin · Traceable to the farmer · Shop on Amazon" in Mulish.
- Put text on a solid deep green or cream panel, or on a dark part of the photo with a soft shade. Never
  over a busy leaf pattern.
- No prices on screen.
- Product shots and the site's own vanilla and landscape photos are fine. No farmers or other
  identifiable people.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter. For short product videos the
  default is no presenter: product, vanilla and type, with a voice-over or music (starter: confirm).
- If Lee appears: lower third "Lee Pearson, founder" and a small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on
  first appearance.

## Voice

Short, warm and confident, like the site. Lead with what is real and where it comes from: "Real vanilla.
Traceable from the shores of Lake Victoria." "Trace every bottle. Down to the farmer who grew it." Name
the product plainly (pure vanilla extract, Grade A beans, bean paste, cocktail bitters). No exclamation
marks, no superlatives.

## Sound

Music is optional. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or Epidemic
Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: the cream logo on deep green `#23331f`, then `bujivanilla.com` in Mulish and one line:
"Real vanilla from Uganda. Shop on Amazon." Hold for two seconds.
