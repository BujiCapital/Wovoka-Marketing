## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **The call to action is "Shop on Amazon".** The site says every order ships through Amazon. Do not
   quote prices or discounts in videos or posts: they are set on Amazon and change.
2. **Say only what the site says about origin:** real, single-origin Ugandan vanilla, grown in
   biodiverse agroforestry along the shores of Lake Victoria, traceable to the farmer who grew it.
   No health claims, no "best", no "only".
3. **No photos or footage of farmers or any identifiable people.** Product, vanilla vines, flowers, beans
   and landscape only. Credit the growers in words, as the site does.
4. **The logo is the "Buji" script.** Write the brand **Buji Vanilla** in text; the logo stands alone.
