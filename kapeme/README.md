## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **A guide, not a shop.** Talk about cafés, roasters and regions the way the articles do. Never imply
   that a café, roaster or brand paid for a mention or endorses Kape.Me (starter: confirm).
2. **Origin facts as the site states them.** The site says the Philippines is "one of the only
   countries" growing all four commercial species. Never upgrade that to "the only country".
3. **Regions, not people.** Regional stories (Cordillera, Kalinga, Sulu and others) use landscapes,
   cups, beans and type. No photos of farmers or community members.
4. **English with a Tagalog line.** The site writes in English and drops in Tagalog the way people talk
   ("Walang kape, walang buhay." "Tara, kape tayo!"). Keep that, and keep it kind.

The call to action, as the site has it: **"Explore Regions"**, and browse the guide at kape.me.
