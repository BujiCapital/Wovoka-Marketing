# How a Kape.Me video looks and sounds

## Colour

From the custom properties in the site's CSS (`index.html` on kape.me).

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Dark ground: hero, footer, end cards | coffee `#1A0F07` (hero gradient also uses `#2C1810`) |
| The brand colour: the dot in "kape.me", buttons, links, section labels | terracotta `#C8553D` (hover `#A03E28`) |
| Eyebrow lines and Tagalog lines on dark | caramel `#D4A574` |
| Region and sustainability tags | forest `#2D6A4F` |
| Page ground | paper `#FAFAF7` (alternate sections `#F0EBE3`) |
| Body text on light | stone `#1C1917` (secondary `#57534E`) |

One terracotta thing per frame: usually the dot. Everything else is white or caramel on coffee, or stone
on paper (starter: confirm).

## Type

- Wordmark and headlines: **Fraunces** (600 for the wordmark; the hero sets "Kape.Me" in light 300 with
  an italic "Kape").
- Body text, labels and buttons: **Inter** (labels uppercase, tracked 0.15em).

Both are free on Google Fonts.

## Logo

Kape.Me has no logo file. The site sets the name as live text: **kape.me** in Fraunces 600, with the dot
in terracotta, in the nav and the footer. The kit's `brand-kit/social/` files are a **starter wordmark,
not a designed logo**, rendered from `brand-kit/kit.json` in the same font and colours. The renderer sets
the dot in the text colour; on a hand-made end card, colour the dot terracotta as the site does.

| File | Use in video |
|---|---|
| `brand-kit/social/logo-on-dark.png` | Starter wordmark on coffee |
| `brand-kit/social/logo-on-light.png` | Starter wordmark on paper |
| `brand-kit/social/profile-1024.png` | "k." monogram for channel avatars |

Written **Kape.Me** in titles and the page title, **kape.me** in the logo. Spoken "kah-peh dot me"
(starter: confirm).

## On-screen text

- Headlines in Fraunces, short. Facts such as elevation and tasting notes in Inter, as the region cards
  have them: `1,200–1,800m elevation · Stone fruit, chocolate`.
- Use a fact only as an article states it, and name the article as the source.
- Landscapes, cups, beans and café interiors with no identifiable people. No photos of farmers or
  community members.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (photos and type under a
  voice-over). For a guide like this the default is no presenter (starter: confirm).
- If Lee appears: lower third "Lee Pearson, founder" and a small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on
  first appearance.

## Voice

Warm, curious and a little cheeky, like a friend who knows every café. English with a Tagalog line where
people would use one. Specific: the region, the species, the elevation, the cup. Short sentences. Kind
about every café and every region.

## Sound

Music is optional. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or Epidemic
Sound); note the licence with the draft.

## Ending every video

End card: **kape.me** in Fraunces on coffee `#1A0F07`, terracotta dot, then one line from the site:
"The ultimate guide to Philippine coffee." and "Tara, kape tayo!" in caramel. Hold for two seconds.
