# How a Wovoka Studio video looks and sounds

Taken from the public brand kit page, https://wovoka.dev/brand, and the site's stylesheet. "Golden hour,
by the rules": an LA sunset meets the Philippine flag, on a surveyor's map.

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Wordmark, highlights, buttons on dark (the flag sun) | sun `#fcd116` |
| Header, dark bands, the icon tile | night `#120d24` |
| Text and art on dark | cream `#fff6e4` |
| Light page background | paper `#fbf6ec` |
| Text on light | ink `#16121f` |
| Links, plate borders, the stripe | flag blue `#1f4fd8` |
| Plate lettering, the stripe | flag red `#d62828` |
| Sunset and sun gradients | mango `#ffb000` |
| Sunset and accent gradients | orange `#ff6b2c` |
| Accent gradient end, the tamaraw's shades | pink `#ff3d7f` |

Two gradients:

- **Sunset**, for heroes, share cards and call-to-action bands. Always top to bottom, night to mango:
  `#120d24 → #2a1450 → #6a1d63 → #b8285c → #ff5e3a → #ffb000`.
- **Accent**, for the one emphasised word in a headline. One per headline: `#fcd116 → #ff6b2c → #ff3d7f`.

Pages are light, on paper, with dark night bands between them.

## Type

Four faces, each with one job. All free on Google Fonts.

- **Bungee**: signs. The wordmark, ROUTE and STOP plates, big figures. Capitals only.
- **Bricolage Grotesque** 800: headlines. Tight tracking (−0.025em), one accent word.
- **Instrument Sans**: body copy, 17 px and up, line height 1.6.
- **IBM Plex Mono**: eyebrows, coordinates, data and sources. Uppercase with wide tracking for labels.

The wordmark is WOVOKA in Bungee with STUDIO beside it in IBM Plex Mono. The icon is the W from the
wordmark on a night tile, over the jeepney stripe (sun, red, blue). Clear space around the wordmark is at
least the width of its W. Minimum size: 120 px wide for the wordmark, 16 px for the icon. On photos or the
sunset, use the on-dark wordmark. Don't stretch it, outline it, retype it in another font or recolour it
outside the palette.

Written **Wovoka Studio**, "part of Wovoka". Spoken "wo-VO-ka studio" (starter: confirm the
pronunciation). The domain is said "wovoka dot dev".

The tamaraw is a small wild buffalo found only on Mindoro. Ours is drawn on a 52 by 44 pixel grid and is
allowed to be funny. Keep it on the grid, scale in whole steps, keep the edges crisp. Give it a one-line
joke in a speech bubble, in plain English or a word of Filipino. Don't smooth it, redraw it, or make it
the butt of a joke about people.

## On-screen text and data

- Eyebrows and labels in IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, wide tracking, in sun on dark.
- Case studies sit on a ROUTE plate, steps in a programme on a STOP plate, in Bungee. Never EXIT.
- Every figure carries its source in Plex Mono: the repository, the database or the date it was counted.
- The coordinates line `8.15° N 125.12° E → 34.05° N 118.24° W` (Mindanao to Los Angeles) can sit top
  right, over faint contour lines.
- Imagery is landscapes, illustration and product screens. Screen recordings of products we still run are
  the best b-roll. No photos of identifiable people from Indigenous partner communities.

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter (type, product screens and the
  tamaraw under a voice-over in Lee's voice).
- Lower third on first appearance: "Lee Pearson, founder".
- A small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on the first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, product or headline on the right. Plain night background or
  the sunset gradient (starter: confirm).

## Voice

Concise, specific, checkable. Show technical range without jargon. Write to an investor who will check
every claim. Lead with the work, then the stack: products we still run beat logos. Short sentences, one
accent word per headline. A light touch of humour is allowed through the tamaraw, never at people's
expense.

## Sound

Music is optional and quiet. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library, Artlist or
Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft. Upbeat, warm, not corporate (starter: confirm).

## Ending every video

End card: the on-dark wordmark on the sunset gradient, then `wovoka.dev` in IBM Plex Mono and one line:
"The studio that runs what it builds." Sign-off below it, smaller: "Built in the Philippines. Cool in Los
Angeles." Hold for two seconds.
