# Carbon Kids marketing

Start here if you are making a video or a post for Carbon Kids (https://carbon.kids). Starter kit,
19 Oct 2026. Everything here is a draft and changes by pull request.

Carbon Kids makes funny comic books and free games about nature for children aged 4 to 8, starting with
the Tammy & Friends series, set in a real forest on Mindoro in the Philippines.

## Rules for this brand

The portfolio rules in the [top-level README](../README.md) apply. On top of them:

1. **Nature first, carbon second.** Lead with the animals and the place. The carbon idea arrives as an
   explanation of something the child already cares about, never as a scare. Do not invert that order.
2. **Book one is still being made.** "Where Did the Shade Go?" is in draft. Say "we are still drawing it"
   or "coming soon". Never say it is out, shipping or on sale until it is.
3. **Animals and landscape only.** The book art shows animals, trees and hills, no people. Keep it that
   way in every video, post and thumbnail, drawn or photographed.
4. **Nothing about real Indigenous figures goes out without Lee.** The planned "People of the land" series
   needs the communities involved in how they are shown. Do not write, illustrate or post anything about it,
   or about any real Indigenous leader, until Lee says so.

## The call to action

"Read the first pages free": a free 5-page sample of book one, emailed from https://carbon.kids. The free
games ("Play Tammy on your TV", https://carbon.kids/play/) are the second call to action.
