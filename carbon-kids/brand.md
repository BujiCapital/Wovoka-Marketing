# How a Carbon Kids video looks and sounds

Taken from the colours, fonts and art on https://carbon.kids. The look is a bright picture book: sky,
grass and sun, thick dark outlines, rounded type.

## Colour

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Wordmark, main buttons, headlines | leaf `#2f7d52` |
| Darker green for small labels and tags | deep leaf `#1f5a3a` |
| The icon's face, second buttons, highlights | sun `#f5b93c` |
| The icon tile, sky in the art | sky `#5aa9d6` |
| Page background | cream `#fffaf0` |
| Text and every outline | ink `#26302a` |
| Occasional accent button | clay `#d97757` |
| Occasional accent | berry `#c8475b` |

The home page hero fades from pale sky `#bfe6f5` through pale green `#e4f5ea` to cream. Use that fade, or
plain cream, behind titles. Outlines are thick and ink coloured, and buttons sit on a hard ink shadow.

## Type

- Headlines, buttons and the characters' speech: **Baloo 2** (800 for headlines).
- Everything else: **Nunito**.

Both are free on Google Fonts. There is no monospace face on this site.

## Logo

| File | Use in video |
|---|---|
| https://carbon.kids/brand/carbon-kids-lockup.svg | The lockup: the CK speech bubble with googly eyes and CARBON KIDS. End cards on cream or pale sky |
| https://carbon.kids/brand/carbon-kids-icon.svg | The CK icon on a sky tile. Corner bug, channel avatars |
| https://carbon.kids/brand/apple-touch-icon.png | Small app icon |

Copies, with PNG exports, are in [`brand-kit/logos/`](brand-kit/logos/). The lockup is green with ink
outlines and is made for light grounds; it does not read on dark green or black.

Written **Carbon Kids** in sentences and **carbon.kids** as the address; the lockup sets it in capitals.
Spoken "carbon kids", and the address "carbon dot kids". The book series is **Tammy & Friends** ("Tammy and
Friends"). Book one is **Where Did the Shade Go?**

The four friends, all real animals or a real tree from Mindoro:

- **Tammy**, a tamaraw (a tiny wild buffalo found only on Mindoro). Charges in first, thinks second.
- **Scoops**, an owl. Knows everything and is asleep for most of it.
- **Ruby**, a dove. Counts everything and always keeps one seed, just in case.
- **Lola Narra**, the oldest tree on the mountain.

## On-screen text and art

- Use the book's own art: the cover, the sample pages and the character drawings. Do not redraw the
  characters or generate new ones in another style.
- Comic speech bubbles with 3 to 8 words, like the book. Sound words in capitals: STOMP!, SPLASH!
- Animals, trees and landscape only. No people, drawn or photographed.
- Facts for grown-ups (for example from the "why we made this" page) carry their source on screen, small,
  in Nunito, with the date (starter: confirm the stamp style).

## The presenter

- **Lee Pearson's own HeyGen avatar and voice only**, or no presenter. For videos aimed at children, use
  no presenter: the book art and the games, with a voice-over (starter: confirm whose voice reads to
  children).
- When Lee appears (videos for parents, teachers and librarians): lower third "Lee Pearson, founder" and a
  small caption "AI avatar of Lee Pearson" on first appearance.
- Framing: head and shoulders, left third, book art on the right, on cream or the pale sky fade.

## Voice

Warm, funny and gentle. Short sentences a new reader can say out loud. Jokes come from the characters:
Tammy charging in, Scoops falling asleep. Real nature, told gently: never frightening, never preachy.
For grown-ups, plain and specific, with the serious reason behind the books and its sources.

## Sound

Music is optional, light and playful. Any track must be licensed for commercial use (HeyGen's library,
Artlist or Epidemic Sound); note the licence with the draft. Comic sound effects (a stomp, a splash, a
hoot) fit the books (starter: confirm).

## Ending every video

End card: the lockup on cream, then `carbon.kids` in Baloo 2 and one line: "Big adventures in a real
forest." Under it, smaller: "Read the first pages free." Hold for two seconds.
